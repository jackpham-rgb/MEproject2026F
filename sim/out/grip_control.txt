Slip limit: F >= SF*m*a/(2 mu) = 0.438 N per finger (SF=3.0, mu=0.6, a=11.65 m/s^2)
Dent limit (ESTIMATE, verify on scrap cans): 8.0 N per finger
Setpoint 1.5 N -> 3.4x above slip limit, 5.3x below dent limit
Window is ~18x wide, so an open-loop servo with compliant pads would likely also work;
the force loop earns its place by (a) confirming the can is actually gripped before the
flip starts and (b) keeping force independent of can diameter/placement error.
